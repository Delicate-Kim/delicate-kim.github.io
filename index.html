About Me

I am from Jeju, Korea, where I grew up exploring mountain and coastal environments. I later studied mechanical engineering at Seoul National University, where fluid mechanics and robotics became my strongest interests.

I am now based in Atlanta, continuing research in fluid simulation and biophysics. Outside the lab, I enjoy playing acoustic guitar as a way to reset and stay creative.

Research Projects

Capillary Flow Simulation with Phase-Field Lattice Boltzmann Solver

Improved a phase-field lattice Boltzmann model to better simulate multiphase capillary flows with large density differences and complex geometries.

Project Page

Lid-Driven Flow in Polygonal Cavities using Lattice Boltzmann Method

Developed a MATLAB implementation of LBM to solve steady-state lid-driven cavity flow problems relevant to coating, aerospace, and thermal systems.

Project Page

Flow Visualization and Drag Calculation using GPU Computing

Simulated high Reynolds-number flow around the Ramblin' Wreck and mosquito-flight cases using a GPU-accelerated LBM pipeline in real time.

Project Page FluidX3D

Wetted Wall Cyclone for Bioaerosol Collector

Investigating aerosol capture in a wetted-wall cyclone to concentrate harmful particles for public health and biosurveillance applications.

Project Page

Kimchi Fermentation in Permeable Korean Earthenware Onggi

Studied how vessel permeability affects fermentation dynamics through time-lapse imaging and carbon dioxide measurements in traditional onggi containers.

Project Page

Human-Robot Hand Mapping Using a Glove-type Sensor

Built a joint-level mapping strategy that translates glove sensor inputs into robotic hand motions for improved dexterous teleoperation.

Project Page

Indentation Simulation of a Skin-like Viscoelastic Material

Modeled skin as a generalized Maxwell material in ABAQUS and proposed a refined Prony-series based method to evaluate viscoelastic behavior.

Project Page

Canine-inspired Unidirectional Flows for Machine Olfaction

Designed and tested dog-inspired airflow pathways to reduce memory effects in odor sensing systems and improve scent-detection stability.
